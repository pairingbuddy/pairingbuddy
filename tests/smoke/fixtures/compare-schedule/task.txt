Add free_slots(bookings, day_start, day_end, min_minutes=15) to schedule.py. It returns the free gaps between day_start and day_end as a list of (start, end) "HH:MM" pairs in time order. bookings is a list of (start, end) "HH:MM" pairs in any order. Bookings that overlap or touch count as one busy block, the parts of a booking outside the day are ignored, and a gap shorter than min_minutes is left out. It raises ValueError when a time is not a valid "HH:MM" from 00:00 to 24:00, when a booking or the day ends before it starts, or when min_minutes is not positive. Example: free_slots([("10:00", "11:00"), ("10:30", "12:00"), ("13:00", "13:10")], "09:00", "14:00") returns [("09:00", "10:00"), ("12:00", "13:00"), ("13:10", "14:00")]. The existing functions and their tests must keep working.
